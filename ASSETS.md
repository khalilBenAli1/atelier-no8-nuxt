# Assets and provenance

All products and this brand are fictional portfolio content. No client assets, real commercial inventory, or guest/customer records are included.

Eight original product photographs were generated in eight separate built-in ImageGen calls on 2026-11-03. Exact prompts are in `docs/image-prompts.json`. Each file corresponds to the named product, in its stated color. Size variants use the same illustrative silhouette; photographs show the Regular proportion and do not document manufactured dimensions.

`public/images/collection-hero*.webp` reuses the approved original three-bag composition from the portfolio, generated on 2026-11-01. Its prompt describes three unbranded leather handbags on pale limestone plinths: caramel tote, espresso top-handle and ivory shoulder bag. No image is hotlinked.

Full product images are 1122×1402px and small variants 600px wide, WebP quality82. Sharp was used for proportional resizing and encoding only. Raw generated masters remain outside the repo. Generated illustrative images are separate from the MIT code license and do not imply real products or a warranty of unique copyright rights.

Typography is locally bundled through locked `@fontsource/playfair-display` and `@fontsource/jost` packages, both under SIL Open Font License1.1. Their complete notices are in `docs/licenses/`. No external font requests occur.
